# SVG builder — banner row rebalance

Parent: [quick edit](svg-builder-quickedit.md).

- **⇔ Rebalance row** (2026-11-15, `lib/svgBuilder/rebalance.ts`, `banner.edgeSpread`; Customize → Banner → text placement, for top/bottom banners — solid, and since 2026-11-15 split (the hero + title evenly inside the accent block, the subtitle centred in the rest) — `builder-banner-rebalance`): one undo step that sets each text's width to its own one line, clears Text padding and the title/subtitle moves, and turns on the spread layout (`banner.ts` `edgeXs`). The row's present parts (hero glyph, title, divider as a zero-width part, subtitle) get EQUAL gaps between each other and both banner edges; the subtitle is no longer capped at 380 px there. **Centred row** (`builder-banner-rebalance-off`) returns to the reference centred group with fixed gaps.
- **Auto rebalance** (2026-11-15, `banner.autoRebalance`): a checkbox beside ⇔ Rebalance row (`builder-banner-auto-rebalance`). Also in the banner's quick bar: **⇔** (`builder-quick-rebalance`) with **Rebalance now** and **Auto rebalance** (`builder-quick-rebalance-now` / `-auto`). While on, `bannerMarkup` applies the rebalance on every draw, for the texts and sizes as they are then, so editing them never breaks the balance; stored widths and moves are ignored meanwhile. Turning it on also applies the rebalance once (one undo step).
- **Rebalance rules** (2026-11-15, replacing the per-part spread on solid edge banners; `rebalance.ts` `fitRow`, `banner.ts` `spreadEdge`):
  1. Title and subtitle each on one line.
  2. Three equal gaps: edge → title → subtitle → edge. Without a subtitle, the title is centred between two equal gaps.
  3. Texts too long: both sizes shrink by the same factor until every gap is at least 5 % of the row (`ROW_MIN_GAP`), and the middle gap also holds the hero. The button writes the fitted sizes (rounded down); drawing fits again whenever the texts change.
  4. The hero glyph, else the divider, sits exactly in the middle of the gap between the texts and follows automatically.
- **Rebalance v3** (2026-11-15, `measure.ts`, `rebalance.ts` `fitBanner`):
  - **Real ink edges.** The texts' real ink boxes are measured with the browser canvas, in the same font, weight and letter spacing the sheet draws; where there is no canvas (Node, tests), a per-character estimate is used. The three equal gaps are measured between those ink edges and the banner edges.
  - **Optimal sizes.** They come from the banner's space. The title starts at 55 % of the banner height (`TITLE_OF_HEIGHT`) and the subtitle at 42 % of the title (`SUB_OF_TITLE`). Both shrink by one factor only as far as the gaps need (at least 5 %). So a tiny subtitle grows and an oversized title shrinks, rounded down to whole pixels.
  - While spread is on, these sizes replace the size sliders; ⇔ writes them into the sliders.
- **Text boxes in ▦ Guides** (2026-11-15, `SvgBuilderTextBoxes.tsx`, `banner.ts` `spreadInkBoxes`): the main toolbar's ▦ Guides toggle, at the guides' opacity, also draws, for a rebalanced banner,, over the artboard, the measured ink boxes of the title and the subtitle (`data-ink`) and the rebalance's gaps with their px (`data-gap`). These are exactly the geometry the drawing used (shared `spreadGeometry`). The separate dev checkboxes are gone. Preview only.
- **Rebalance proportions** (2026-11-15, `banner.rowGapPct` 1–30 % / `titleFillPct` 20–95 % / `subRatioPct` 15–100 %, defaults 5 / 55 / 42; `SvgBuilderRowProportions.tsx`): sliders beside Auto rebalance (`builder-banner-row-gap` / `-title-fill` / `-sub-ratio`, and `builder-quick-…` in the ⇔ panel). They set the smallest gap as a % of the banner width (bigger gaps, smaller texts), how tall the title may be as a % of the banner height, and the subtitle's size as a % of the title. `fitBanner` reads them in place of the constants.
- **Rebalance, Y and split** (2026-11-15): each rebalanced text's ink box is centred on the banner's middle line (`inkCentredTop`, from the measured ascent and descent). Split edge banners reuse the solid row exactly (`spreadGeometry`): optimal sizes and three equal gaps. The seam between the two colours takes the divider's place, exactly in the middle of the gap between the texts; the hero, when on, sits centred on the seam, and the title is drawn in the accent's readable ink. Dev boxes show for solid and split edge banners alike. The row helpers moved to `bannerrow.ts`.
- **Centred halves** (2026-11-15): the gap between the title and the subtitle is now **twice** the edge gaps (edge g · title · 2g · subtitle · g), so each text sits centred in its half. The hero / divider / colour seam sits at the middle of that gap. This applies to solid and split banners alike; the Gap % setting is the edge gap's minimum.
- **⇔ on every banner** (2026-11-16; columns 2026-11-17, `offersRebalance`): the rebalance button and panel (Rebalance now, Auto rebalance, proportions) appear in the quick bar of the banner **and** of a split banner's coloured block, for every banner but a plain grid. On a left / right banner the button reads **⇔ Rebalance column**; only a plain grid has nothing to rebalance.
- **The column rule** (2026-11-17, `rebalance.ts` `fitColumn`, `banner.ts` `columnGeometry`): a left / right banner carries the same principle with the axes swapped. The distances run VERTICALLY between the texts' real ink BLOCKS (wrapped lines included): edge g · title · 2g · subtitle · g over the banner's height; every line's ink is centred on the column's middle line; the hero's double ring — solid, and on a split block in the block's readable ink, the column being tall enough for it — else the divider, sits exactly in the middle of the double gap, and a split banner's colour seam ends there. The user's sizes stay, shrunk by ONE factor only when the gaps would fall below the Gap % of the height (the middle gap also holds the hero); `titleFillPct` / `subRatioPct` size rows only.
- **The rule is the default** (2026-11-17): new designs start with `banner.autoRebalance` on (`DEFAULT_CONFIG`, every layout preset), and every saved template whose banner is a row or a column migrates to it on read (`templates.ts` `lookOf`), so applying an old template still lays its texts by the rule. A stored sheet keeps an explicit opt-out; **Auto rebalance** off plus **Centred row** returns the reference centred group with fixed gaps.
- **Column boxes in ▦ Guides**: the ink blocks and the three gaps draw as for the row, the gap bars vertical on the middle line with their px beside them.
